External Ticket Raising & Live Tracking Portal

A customer-facing support portal. A client opens their project's public URL, raises a support ticket, receives an acknowledgement number by email, and tracks the ticket live until it is resolved or closed. This document maps every screen, decision, error and hand-off a real person encounters, end to end.

Version: 1.0.0 Last updated: 25 Sep 2026 No login required · Public portal

📍 User Journey — Plain-English Summary

  1. Customer opens their branch or dealership's support link (e.g. /SBI) — no account or login needed.
  2. Customer fills in the Raise Ticket form and submits it directly (an OTP identity check is planned here, not yet built).
  3. The system validates every field, checks for a predefined/known issue (emailing a solution document if one matches), and creates the ticket as Pending.
  4. Customer receives an acknowledgement number on screen — this is their only "login," used together with their email for every future lookup.
  5. The ticket is routed to the respective Project Coordinator's dashboard in the DSS Issue Tracker.
  6. When the coordinator opens the ticket, its status auto-changes from Pending to Acknowledged, and an acknowledgement email is sent.
  7. Behind the scenes, staff log into the DSS Issue Tracker (Trackwise) — session-based login with an optional project-based OTP — and see the ticket on their Dashboard and Ticket List.
  8. A Manager (Trackwise "Admin") or SuperAdmin reviews and approves it, moving its internal status to In Progress; the approval is synchronized outward to an external ticket system.
  9. Support Staff then work the ticket — uploading attachments and adding any number of remarks, especially if resolution takes longer — all visible to the customer on their tracking timeline.
  10. Each meaningful status change and remark is reflected on the customer's tracking timeline; resolution/closed emails include a direct tracking link.
  11. Customer can return any time and enter the ack number + email to see live status — never anyone else's ticket, and never editable once submitted.
  12. When resolved or closed, the customer sees the fix summary, proof files and total time taken — the journey ends here; a new issue needs a new ticket.

📊 Journey Statistics

By design — not part of this journey: customer login / registration, password reset, account lockout, and a staff/admin dashboard inside this portal. Support staff and managers work entirely inside the existing DSS Issue Tracker; this portal is create-and-track only for customers. OTP identity verification is planned, not yet built — see the Business Rules section below. Where a rule was not specified for this build, it is marked "Business Rule Required."

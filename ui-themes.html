26 · Editorial / Magazine
Cover Story

The Last Light of the Long Summer

Words by Mira Okafor · Photography by Jonas Reyes · 14 min read
There is a moment, just before the sun commits to the horizon, when the whole coastline seems to hold its breath. Fishermen call it the pause. Photographers call it the only hour that matters. We spent eleven days on the northern shore chasing it, learning that the best light is rarely the loudest — it arrives sideways, patient, gold at the edges and blue at the centre. What follows is a portrait of a place that refuses to be hurried, told through the people who still arrange their lives around the tide and the sky above it.
